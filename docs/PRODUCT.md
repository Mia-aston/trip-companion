# Product definition

## Core promise

Give a traveller one evolving workspace that knows the trip constraints and surfaces the right planning, knowledge and recommendations at the right moment.

## Primary flow

### Before
- create trip: origin, dates, destination or open-ended destination search;
- compare transport;
- save flight/accommodation;
- generate editable itinerary;
- surface visa/logistics/tasks;
- generate "learn before you go" cards;
- estimate and split budget.

### During
- show today timeline;
- adjust itinerary when plans change;
- surface nearby food, practical guidance and photo spots;
- show short context cards for the place the traveller is visiting;
- add actual expenses quickly.

### After
- reconcile costs and settlement;
- show visited places;
- create trip recap and reusable preferences.

## Recommendation principles

- Recommendations must explain *why* they fit the user and current itinerary.
- Live facts (price, hours, availability, transit) come from providers.
- LLMs rank/compose normalized facts; they are not the authoritative database.
- Food matching uses declared taste and textual review signals, not inferred ethnicity/race.
